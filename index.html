Webpage Introduction



This is a webpage created by Group 2, for the Lifelabs Q1 IPBA. In here, we will put the summary of our topic and our answers to the questions on the provided answer sheet.

There are 4 learning areas of Lifelabs or TLE. they are the Information and Communications, or ICT, Agricultural and Fishery Arts, or AFA, Family and Consumer Science, or FCS, and lastly Industrial Arts, or IA. these 4 learning areas serve not just to make our lives easier, but also to implement innovations that can help with general communication, sustainabiity practices, improving hygiene, and industrial safety.

Some widespread innovations utilized within these 4 areas of TLE respectfully are the following:

Information and Communications

  • Machine Translators (1954)
  • Internet (1983)
  • Intergrated Circuit or IC (1958)
  • Email (1971)

Agricultural and Fishery Arts

  • Vertical Farming (1915)
  • Modern Hydroponics (1920s)
  • Automated Feeding Systems (1980s)

Family and Consumer Science

  • Dishwashers and Washing Machines
  • Food Processing and Preserving
  • Sustainable Textiles and Materials

Industrial Arts

  • 3D Printing (1980s)
  • Robotics (1940-1950s)
  • Artificial Intelligence/AI (1950)

These inventions not only helps people in terms of efficiency, space-saving, and sustainability, it also highlights how far these 4 area of Lifelabs or TLE have come, from simple solutions, to more complex and wide-ranged solutions for different problems.
